Google's Gemini text-to-speech models, one speaker: `gemini-3-8-flash-lite-tts`, `gemini-3-8-flash-tts`, `gemini-3-1-flash-tts` and `gemini-2-5-pro-tts` join `TTS_PROVIDERS` and the catalog (maker `google`, mode `tts`); ElevenLabs v4 stays the default. A speech model's capability sheet (`TtsCapabilities`) gains optional fields, absent on every existing model: `voices` and `defaultVoice` (a model that speaks only its own named voices — 70 on 3.8, grouped by use; 30 on 3.1 / 2.5), `tagSyntax: "angle"` and `tags` (3.8 writes its tone tags `<laugh>`, `<short pause>`, … from a closed list), `direction` (`TtsDirectionSheet`: free-text `styleDirection` on 3.8; a style, pace, accent and audio profile on 3.1 / 2.5) and `ratePer1kChars` (the model's length price is quoted per 1,000 characters). New helpers from `@nodaro/shared/model-catalog`: `ttsNamedVoices`, `ttsAcceptsVoice`, `ttsNamedVoiceFor`, `ttsVoiceForModel` (the voice a run on a model speaks when the model and the voice were chosen apart: a Gemini model's default for a voice not on its list, none for a Gemini voice on an ElevenLabs model), `ttsDirection`, `ttsDirectionFor`, `ttsDirectionProblem`, `ttsAngleTags`, and the types `TtsNamedVoice`, `TtsDirectionSheet`, `TtsDirectionValues`, `GeminiTtsVoiceGroup`. From `@nodaro/shared/models`: `GEMINI_TTS_VOICE_GROUPS`, `GEMINI_3_8_TTS_VOICES`, `GEMINI_3_1_TTS_VOICES`, `GEMINI_3_8_DEFAULT_VOICE`, `GEMINI_3_1_DEFAULT_VOICE`, `GEMINI_3_8_TONE_TAGS`, `GEMINI_3_1_TTS_STYLES`, `GEMINI_3_1_TTS_PACES`, `GEMINI_3_1_TTS_ACCENTS`, `GEMINI_TTS_MAX_CHARS`, `TTS_DIRECTION_MAX_CHARS`.

Length pricing learns a rate row quoted per 1,000 characters, `<model>:per-1k-chars` (`SPEECH_PER_1K_CREDIT_SUFFIX`): the count is unchanged (started 100 characters, at least 8) and a request costs its hundreds × the row ÷ 10, rounded up once. `speechUnitCreditId(model)` names the row a model is charged at (per 100 or per 1,000), `speechRowUnits(model)` how many hundreds one row prices (1 or 10), `speechRowCredits(units, row, rowUnits)` and `speechCreditsFor(model, chars, row)` the base of a request. A request's quote id keeps one shape for every model, `<model>:per-100-chars:<N>u` (N in started hundreds), so a client that builds it with `speechQuoteCreditId(model, speechPriceUnits(chars))` quotes the new models unchanged; `parseSpeechQuoteCreditId` now also answers `rowUnits`, and `isSpeechLengthPriceId` / `speechLengthPriceModel` read a `:per-1k-chars` row too.
