Social Search: `socialSearchPostLink` reads the page link of the post a Social Search wire hands on (one post, or the first of a list), as Video Analysis reads it. The page price is 20 credits per page of up to 20 results (`SOCIAL_SEARCH_CREDITS_PER_PAGE`), so `social-search:1` / `:2` / `:3` cost 20 / 40 / 60.
