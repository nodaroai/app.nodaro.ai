Video Face Mesh: `VIDEO_FACE_MESH_PARTS` gains `"contours"`, which draws the face oval, both eyebrows, the nose and the eye and lip outlines, with no wireframe. `VideoFaceMeshParts`, `VideoFaceMeshRequest.parts` and `VideoFaceMeshResponse.parts` take it. `"full"` stays the default.
