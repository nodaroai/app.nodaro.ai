`client.credits.usageSummary()` sums a period's credit usage the way a billing screen draws it: totals (used, purchased, granted, refunded, runs, the average per run), the spend by model and by category, a series over time by day, week or month with no holes, and the spend by session and by workflow. The CLI gains `nodaro billing summary`.
