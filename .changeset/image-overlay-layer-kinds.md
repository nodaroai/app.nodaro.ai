Image Overlay layer kinds: the shared wire contract for text (bundled fonts, weight, size as % of the base, outline, background box), QR and shape layers (eleven flat shapes with one geometry shared by the editor and the server), plus image finishing effects (circle mask, feather, stroke, glow), and platform presets with safe areas and per-device zones (the YouTube banner's TV / desktop / all-devices viewports).
