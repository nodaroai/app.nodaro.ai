Published apps, components and templates carry the "up to" end of their listed price range: `maxEstimatedCredits`, `maxPerMinuteCredits` and `maxPerItemCredits` on an app or component, `maxEstimatedCredits` and `maxEstimatedPerMinuteCredits` on a template — the most a run can cost over the choices its user has. Present only when they differ from the "from" figures.
