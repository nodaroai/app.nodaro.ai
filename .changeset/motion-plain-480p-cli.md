`nodaro trends estimate|run` take `--draft`: a 480p draft (Nodaro Motion Prism trends only) that Full Quality can finish at 1080p. Without it a run is plain, at 480p too; `--no-draft` is still accepted and sends `draft: false`. `nodaro cast-swap full-quality` now describes Full Quality as finishing a Prism draft only: Nodaro Motion Pulse runs have none.
