Video Remake: `resolution` is the size the video is delivered at. `VideoRemakeParams.resolution` takes the new `VideoRemakeResolution` (`"480p" | "720p" | "1080p" | "4k"`): Nodaro Prism and Nodaro Pulse deliver `"720p"` (the default), `"1080p"` or `"4k"`, their Flash choices `"720p"` (the default) or `"1080p"`. `videoRemake.styleChoice()` answers `resolutions`, the sizes each engine choice offers on the deployment, smallest (the default) first; list exactly those. A size the engine choice does not offer is refused 400 `resolution_not_offered` (the error's `offered` names the sizes it does), and an offered size a request cannot have is refused 409 `resolution_unavailable`, before anything is held. `VideoRemakeEstimate.resolution` and `VideoRemakeJobOutput.resolution` are the size delivered. Where `resolutions` is empty, the deployment's engine does not deliver resolutions yet and `resolution` is the size it makes, as before. `VideoToVideoRemakeParams.resolution` (`nodes.run("video-to-video", …)` with a Video Remake engine) takes the same sizes.

The upscale choice is removed from the types: `VideoRemakeParams.upscale`, the estimate's `outputResolution`, `upscale`, `upscaleOffered` and `breakdown.upscale`, the job output's `outputResolution`, `upscale`, `engineVideoUrl`, `engineWidth`, `engineHeight` and `upscaleCharged`, `styleChoice()`'s `upscaleChoice` and `upscaleTo`, and the types `VideoRemakeUpscaleChoice`, `VideoRemakeUpscaleOutcome`, `VideoRemakeOutputResolution` and `VideoRemakeUpscaleTo`. Code that names them no longer type-checks: send `resolution` alone, the size you want delivered, and read `resolution` back. Fields sent beyond the typed ones still reach the server as before.

`UserBalance.firstRemakeOffer` gains `allowedDelivered`: the runs "First Remake on us" covers named by the size delivered (a 4-second preview at 720p on Nodaro Prism or Nodaro Pulse), present only where the deployment's engine delivers resolutions. `allowed` keeps naming them by the engine's size (Nodaro Prism 480p, Nodaro Pulse 720p), for requests and engines that use it.
