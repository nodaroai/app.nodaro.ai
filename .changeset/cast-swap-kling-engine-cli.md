`nodaro cast-swap full-quality estimate` says whether the Full Quality is the same take at 1080p or, for a Kling 4 run at 720p, a new take, and lists every part of the quote. `nodaro trends get` shows the trend's engine; `--resolution` takes one the trend offers (default its first: 480p, or 720p on a Kling 4 trend, which has no draft).
