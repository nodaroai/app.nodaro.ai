Typed welcome offer. `UserBalance` gains `welcomeOffer` (`{ popupSeen, consentPending, credits }`, present only while the deployment has the offer on), `freeGrantState` and `freeGrantNotice`; `welcomeOffer.credits` is the amount a claim grants, sent by the server, so an app shows that figure instead of a hard-coded number that goes stale when the grant changes. Two new helpers replace raw `client.request(...)` calls: `client.credits.claimWelcomeOffer({ sourceApp, browserKey, deviceKey })` (records the email consent and claims the grant; resolves `{ consent, grant, credits }`) and `client.credits.markWelcomeOfferSeen()` (the one-time popup was shown; resolves `{ ok: true }`). New exported types: `FreeGrantState`, `WelcomeOfferState`, `WelcomeOfferClaimInput`, `WelcomeOfferClaimResult`, `WelcomeOfferSeenResult`. Both routes are browser-session only: they work from a first-party web app signed in with a Supabase session and answer 403 to an API key or OAuth app token, and 409 `welcome_offer_disabled` while the offer is off. To adopt: read the amount from `balance().welcomeOffer.credits` and swap your raw calls for the two helpers; nothing else changes.
