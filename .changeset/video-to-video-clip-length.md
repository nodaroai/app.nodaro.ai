Video to Video on HappyHorse Edit and Wan 2.7 VideoEdit is priced by the length of the clip it edits, as the server reads it before anything is held. New exports from `@nodaro/shared/credits`: `videoToVideoClipPrice` (the row a run reserves and, for a rate, its units; Wan 2.7 VideoEdit is charged the larger of its flat row and its per-second rate), `checkVideoToVideoClipLength` (HappyHorse Edit takes 3–60 s, Wan 2.7 VideoEdit 2–10 s), `videoToVideoBilledSeconds` (HappyHorse Edit: every started half-second; Wan 2.7 VideoEdit: whole seconds of the clip it delivers), `videoToVideoEstimatePrice` / `videoToVideoEstimateSeconds` (an estimate quotes the model's longest clip when the length is not known), `videoToVideoSentResolution` (HappyHorse Edit runs and is priced at 720p unless 1080p is named), `videoToVideoClipRateOf`, `wanVideoEditRateFromSeconds`, `isClipPricedV2vProvider` and the `CLIP_PRICED_V2V_PROVIDERS`, `V2V_CLIP_RATES`, `V2V_CLIP_RATE_IDS`, `HAPPYHORSE_EDIT_*`, `WAN_VIDEOEDIT_*` constants. Three new four-second rows are listed in the catalog entries' pricing: `happyhorse-edit:720p:per-4s`, `happyhorse-edit:1080p:per-4s` and `wan-videoedit:1080p:per-4s`; the existing rows are unchanged.
