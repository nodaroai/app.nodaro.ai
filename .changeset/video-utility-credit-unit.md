The video-utility credit estimators (Trim Video, Loop Video, Combine Videos, Assemble Narrated Video) return their prices in the current credit unit: 10 credits per 5 seconds of output, per ~24 frames searched, per combine input beyond two, and per Assemble step. They returned the pre-redenomination figure, a tenth of the listed price. `VIDEO_UTIL_PRICING.CREDIT_UNIT` names the unit.
