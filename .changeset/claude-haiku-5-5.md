Claude Haiku 5.5 joins the LLM registry as `claude-haiku-5.5` (economy tier): the first Haiku with reasoning effort levels (`low` to `max`). It reasons by default (`thinkingDefaultOn`), does not accept `temperature`, and binds its thinking blocks to the conversation (`conversationBoundThinking`). Claude Haiku 4.5 stays available; its picker description now reads "Previous Haiku".
