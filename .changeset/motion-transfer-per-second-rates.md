Motion Transfer is charged per second of its motion video on every model and size. `MOTION_TRANSFER_SECOND_RATES` now gives Kling 2.6 (720p, 1080p), Kling 3.0 (720p, 1080p), Wan Animate Replace (480p, 580p, 720p) and Wan Animate Move (720p) a four-second row each (`motion-transfer:720p:per-4s`, `motion-transfer:1080p:per-4s`, `kling-3.0-motion:1080p:per-4s`, `wan-animate-replace:480p:per-4s`, `wan-animate-replace:580p:per-4s`, `wan-animate-replace:720p:per-4s`, `wan-animate-move:720p:per-4s`), listed in the catalog entries' pricing. Wan Animate is never charged below its flat price for the size: a rate names its `floorId`, and the new `motionTransferChargedPrice` / `motionTransferBaseCharge` pick the row a run is charged at (the floor row once when it is at least the per-second price, else the rate row × its units). `motionTransferRateOf` now always answers a rate (a model Motion Transfer does not have is priced as Kling 2.6), `motionTransferPrice` carries the `floorId`, and `MOTION_TRANSFER_RATE_IDS` lists each row once; `MOTION_TRANSFER_FLOOR_IDS` is new. Wan Animate Move runs at 720p, the only size it supports: its catalog entry lists `720p` alone, so `motionTransferResolution` (and `buildMotionCreditModelIdentifier`) move a 480p or 580p Move run to 720p.
