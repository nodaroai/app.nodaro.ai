A render's saved output has one reader: `savedRenderOutput(data)` (the selected result, as the node's medium) and `savedRenderBatch(data)` / `savedRenderBatchUrls(data)` (the latest batch only, row-aligned, never the result history). Each item carries what its render stamped: `quality` (`"proxy"` is a Preview) and `clipKey`. Also `renderResultStamp(output)`, `edlSpanKey(edl)` (a plan clip's identity, `${min inMs}-${max outMs}`), `planClipKeyAt(plan, row)`, `renderPlanPath(renderId, nodes, edges)` / `renderPlanNodeId(...)` (the Edit Plan behind a render's `edl` input, through teleports and Camera Switch, with the wires whose selectors pick its clips), `renderClipKey(plan, row, hops?)`, `renderPlanClipKey(renderId, nodes, edges, planClips, row)` (the clip key a render iteration stamps — row k of the clips every selector on the way picks, or the one clip a wire that hands on one value picks: `item`, `item:N`, `all` leaving one clip, or an Edit Plan's Selected output, its first clip), and the `RenderGraphNode` / `RenderGraphEdge`, `RenderPlanHop` / `RenderPlanPath`, `RenderQuality`, `RenderMedium`, `RenderResultStamp`, `RunResultRowStamp` and `SavedRenderItem` types. Additive.
