Video Remake plans roles from the prompt: `videoRemake.planRoles({ videoUrl, prompt, images, engine?, roles?, references?, preview?, previewStart? })` (`POST /v1/video-remake/plan-roles`, free, signed in, 6 calls a minute and 60 a day per account) reads a request in the person's own words and 1–30 loose reference images (the request may be empty or left out: the images alone are enough) and answers `{ prompt, roles, decisions?, references, postPrompt, removed, unresolved }` (`VideoRemakePlannedRoles`): the new roles and fixed references exactly as `run()` and `estimate()` take them, the rest of the request as the run's `postPrompt`, every image it could not place with why (`unresolved`), and how each role was decided (`decisions`, one per role: `{ role, basis: "request" | "inferred", evidence?, confidence?: "clear" | "guess", alternatives? }`). `styleChoice()` answers `planRoles` (one boolean): offer it only where it is `true`. New types, also from `@nodaro/sdk/video-remake`: `VideoRemakePlanRolesParams`, `VideoRemakePlannedRoles`, `VideoRemakePlanRolesUnresolved`, `VideoRemakePlanRolesUnresolvedReason`, `VideoRemakePlanRolesDecision` and `VideoRemakePlanRolesRefusalCode` (`plan_roles_not_available`, `image_unreadable`, `clip_out_of_range`, `preview_out_of_range`, `video_unreadable`, `plan_roles_failed`).
