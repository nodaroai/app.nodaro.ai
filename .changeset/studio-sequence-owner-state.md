A linked (keyframe / sequence) studio production's owner state now reaches only its owner (studio ruling T87). New owner-only key lists, one per level they live at: `STUDIO_TAKE_SEQUENCE_KEYS` (`sequenceEndpoints` — a linked clip take's keyframe endpoint pins, on a node's `data.generatedResults` rows), `STUDIO_SEQUENCE_UNIT_MANIFEST_KEYS` (`requestManifest` — a unit result's frozen request, on a unit video node's `data.sequenceUnitResults` rows), `STUDIO_KEYFRAME_NODE_TRANSIENT_KEYS` (`keyframePendingImages` — a keyframe's image runs in flight, on its image node's `data`), `STUDIO_SEQUENCE_PLANNING_KEYS` (`sequenceRecommendations` — the director's sequence recommendations, on `settings.studio`), `STUDIO_SEQUENCE_POLICY_KEYS` (`sequenceGenerationPolicies` — the owner's current generation preferences, on `settings.studio`; EMPTIED to `{}`, never dropped and never added, because the studio codec's reader requires the record on a production with takes), `STUDIO_SEQUENCE_UNIT_REVIEW_KEYS` (`continuationAcceptance` — a sequence take unit's continuation review, on `settings.studio.sequenceTakes[].units[]`), `STUDIO_KEYFRAME_REVIEW_KEYS` (`rejections` — a keyframe's review record, on `settings.studio.keyframes[]` and on a deleted keyframe in the bin) and `STUDIO_KEYFRAME_TRANSIENT_KEYS` (`pendingImages` — a deleted keyframe's runs in flight, in the bin).

`stripStudioTakeVoiceRecords` now also drops the pins, the unit results' frozen requests and the keyframe runs from every node; `stripStudioDraftSettings` (the `view` read) and `stripStudioTransientSettings` (the public share read) drop the recommendations, the unit reviews and the keyframe rejections and empty the preferences; `stripStudioDraftWorkflow` and the recycle bin's deleted takes, keyframes and scenes get the same. What the codec's reader cannot do without stays: a take's `policy` (the one it was made with) and `compilation`, a unit result's pin, url and request hash, and a keyframe's plan, acceptance and result history.
