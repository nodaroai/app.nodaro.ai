Transition `zoom-into-mirror` gets a new description. The camera pushes straight at a mirror in the frame; as the lens meets the glass the mirror's surface turns liquid and rings ripple out across the picture, and the camera keeps moving forward through the rippling surface into the second shot. The mirror keeps its own reflection until the lens touches the glass, and the shot ends in the second shot, still, with no ripples left. No other row changes.
