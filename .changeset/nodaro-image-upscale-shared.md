Nodaro Image Upscale (`nodaro-image-upscale`): a new image upscaler on Upscale Image and `/v1/edit-image`, at 1x (enhance only), 2x, 4x or 8x, that restores detail and keeps lettering exactly as written. It joins `IMAGE_EDIT_PROVIDERS`, `UPSCALE_IMAGE_PROVIDERS` and `MODEL_CATALOG`, offered under the deployment's own label (`ownLabel: true`, family "Nodaro Upscale"), and is listed only where a deployment offers it. It is priced by the output's size: `nodaro-image-upscale:2K` / `:4K` / `:8K` for an output up to 2048², 4096² and 8192² pixels, and the bare `nodaro-image-upscale` for a run whose image size is not known before it starts. Adds `UPSCALE_FACTORS` / `UpscaleFactor` (every factor an upscaler takes: "1", "2", "4", "8"), `NODARO_IMAGE_UPSCALE_FACTORS`, `NODARO_IMAGE_UPSCALE_DEFAULT_FACTOR`, `NODARO_IMAGE_UPSCALE_MODEL_ID`, `resolveNodaroImageUpscaleFactor`, `upscaleFactorsFor` (the factors one model takes), `resolveUpscaleFactor` (the factor a request renders at: Topaz's through `resolveTopazUpscale`, any other model's as given, except "8", which only Nodaro Image Upscale takes and is never sent to another model) and `snappedUpscaleFactor` (the factor a node must carry after a switch to a model that does not take its stored one, or `null` when an "8" is left on a model with no factor list and is cleared), all on `@nodaro/shared/credits`. Topaz's factors are unchanged.
