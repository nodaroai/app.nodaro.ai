Transitions `sun-glare`, `lens-crack` and `lightning-flash` get new descriptions. The glare floods the lens from one corner until the picture washes out, then clears to the second shot; the crack spreads into a web of fracture lines on the lens while everything behind it stays whole; the lightning bolt's flash turns the whole picture white and the second shot appears as the white fades. The camera and framing stay put in all three. No other row changes.
