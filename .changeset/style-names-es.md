The art style catalog's Spanish translation now names every style, in sentence case. It keeps the loanwords Spanish speakers use ("Anime", "Noir", "Cyberpunk", "Steampunk", "Vaporwave", "Ukiyo-e", "Pixel art", "Pop art", "Concept art", "Low poly", "Glitch art") as explicit names, and gives "Aguada de tinta", "Animación con plastilina", "Arte ASCII" and "Plano técnico" for ink wash, claymation, ASCII art and blueprint.
