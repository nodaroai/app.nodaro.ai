Documentation only: the doc comments of a Video Remake role's `subject` (`VideoRemakeRolePrevizSettings`) and of `VideoRemakeClipKind` now say that on a 3D previz run `subject` is what the role's model becomes in the finished video, written as that finished thing rather than as the previz model and without the model's colour unless the real thing has it (`"a jockey on a bay racehorse"`, not `"the driver of the red car"`). No types or behaviour change.
