`GenerateImageParams` and `ImageToImageParams` type `outputFormat` (`png`, `jpeg`, `webp`, `gif`, `svg`), `gifFrames`, `gifFrameDurationMs` and `svgLevel`, on the models that list them (GPT Image 2.6 Flare and Sunburst). `ModelSummary` carries `outputFormats`, `gifFrameSize`, `gifFrames`, `gifFrameDurationMs` and `svgLevels`.
