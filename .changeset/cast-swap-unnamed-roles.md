Nodaro Motion: a person, pet or product role of a direct run may leave out its `name` (`CastSwapPersonRole.name`, `CastSwapPetRole.name`, `CastSwapProductRole.name` are now optional). An unnamed role is named by its first picture and needs at least one image (400 `unnamed_role_needs_picture` otherwise); a trend run's roles are always named.
