Google's Gemini speech models voice two-speaker dialogue: `gemini-3-8-flash-lite-tts`, `gemini-3-8-flash-tts`, `gemini-3-1-flash-tts` and `gemini-2-5-pro-tts` join `DIALOGUE_PROVIDERS` under the same ids (their catalog `modes` are now `["tts", "dialogue"]`, one sheet and one price row for both); ElevenLabs v3 dialogue stays the default. `TtsCapabilities` gains `dialogueSpeakers` (the most distinct voices a script may use: 2 on Gemini) and `fillerWords` (3.8: filler words between two speakers on request). New dialogue helpers from `@nodaro/shared/models`: `dialogueMaxSpeakers`, `dialogueTakesFillerWords`, `dialogueVoicesOf`, `dialogueVoicesForModel` (each line's voice when the model and the voices were chosen apart: a Gemini model keeps the voices it speaks and moves the others onto its voices the script does not use yet, so two speakers stay two; an ElevenLabs model drops a Gemini voice), `dialogueLineStyleMaxChars` / `dialogueLineStyleFor` (3.8: a free-text style per line), `dialogueTakesSpeakerDirection` / `dialogueSpeakerDirectionFor` (3.1 / 2.5: a style, pace, accent and audio profile per speaker), `dialogueProblem` (the one check every dialogue door runs: the speaker limit, the model's own voices, the line style's length, a speaker's choices), `ELEVENLABS_DIALOGUE_MAX_VOICES`, and the types `DialogueRequestShape`, `DialogueProblem`. A dialogue's file: `TtsCapabilities.dialogueAnyOutputFormat` (Gemini: every `TTS_OUTPUT_FORMATS`), `dialogueOutputFormats(provider)` (the ElevenLabs dialogue models: the default MP3 only) and `dialogueOutputFormatFor(provider, value)`; `dialogueProblem` refuses a format the model does not deliver.
