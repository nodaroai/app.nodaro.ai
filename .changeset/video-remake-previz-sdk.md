Video Remake takes a 3D previz clip: `videoRemake.run()` and `videoRemake.estimate()` take `clipKind: "footage" | "previz"` (`VideoRemakeClipKind`; left out: an ordinary clip, as before). A previz clip (simple untextured models moved and filmed as the video to make will be) is rendered into the run's look instead of edited: its art style or custom style, else real-world live-action footage. On such a run each role's `name` is the locator of its model in the clip (`"the pink model"`), its new optional `subject` says what it is (`"the detective"`), and `previz: { lighting?: boolean, sound?: "clip" | "generated" }` (`VideoRemakePrevizOptions`) says whether the clip's lighting is followed and whose sound the result has. Nodaro Prism only, no draft and no Full Quality. `styleChoice()` answers `previz` (one boolean): offer it only where it is `true`. New types, also from `@nodaro/sdk/video-remake`: `VideoRemakeClipKind`, `VideoRemakePrevizOptions`, `VideoRemakeRolePrevizSettings` and `VideoRemakePrevizRefusalCode` (`previz_not_available`, `previz_needs_prism`, `previz_not_for_trends`, `previz_no_draft`, `previz_role_needs_locator`, `previz_needs_clip_kind`, `previz_no_full_quality`).
