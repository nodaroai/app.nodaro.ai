`RESTYLE_PREVIEWS` (`@nodaro/prompts/picker-art`): for an art style in the `style` catalog, a short clip of real footage wiping into that style, for a picker that restyles a video. An entry exists only for an approved clip, and can name the moment its still is cut from. `restylePreviewStillUrl` gives that still, and `lookPreviewStillUrl` takes an optional time for a clip (default 1 s, so every existing still is the same URL). `LOOK_PREVIEW_SETS.style` and the public catalogs do not change.
