Nodaro Video Upscale, a new Video Upscale provider: `nodaro-video-upscale` joins `VIDEO_UPSCALE_PROVIDERS` and the catalog (`ownLabel`, kind `video`, mode `video-upscale`, resolutions `1080p` / `2k` / `4k`, priced per second of the clip through one row per tier that prices four seconds). It upscales a clip of up to 60 seconds and 50 MB into a 1080p, 2K or 4K frame, keeping its shape, frame rate, length and sound. New exports from `@nodaro/shared/models`: `NODARO_VIDEO_UPSCALE_PROVIDER`, `NODARO_VIDEO_UPSCALE_TIERS`, `NODARO_VIDEO_UPSCALE_DEFAULT_TIER`, `NODARO_VIDEO_UPSCALE_BOX`, `NODARO_VIDEO_UPSCALE_MAX_SEC`, `NODARO_VIDEO_UPSCALE_MAX_BYTES`, `isNodaroVideoUpscaleTier`, `isNodaroVideoUpscaleProvider` and the `NodaroVideoUpscaleTier` type. From `@nodaro/shared/model-catalog`: `VIDEO_UPSCALE_RESOLUTIONS` (every provider's targets), `videoUpscaleModelOf`, `videoUpscaleResolutionsFor` and `normalizeVideoUpscaleResolution`, which coerces a Video Upscale target to one its provider takes for a provider whose catalog entry declares its resolutions (today Nodaro Video Upscale; a value it does not take becomes its 1080p default); `normalizeNodeModelParams` applies it to `video-upscale` nodes on that provider and leaves every other provider's node as it is.
