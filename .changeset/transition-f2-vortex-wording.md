Transitions `sand-storm`, `paint-splash`, `aurora-sweep` and `vortex-swirl` get new descriptions. The three element rows cover the whole picture from the front with the material (sand, paint, aurora light) while the camera and framing stay put, then clear to reveal the second shot, ending with none of the material left. In `vortex-swirl` only the first subject twists into a narrow column at its place in the frame, and the column unwinds into the second subject while the frame stays level. No other row changes.
