Cast Swap face masking (admin-set) chooses what a mask covers: `faceMasking.target`, `"head"` (the default: the whole head, hair included, and people seen from behind) or `"face"` (the face only; the hair stays visible).

`@nodaro/shared/models`: `CAST_SWAP_FACE_MASK_TARGETS` (`["face", "head"]`), the `CastSwapFaceMaskTarget` type, and `CAST_SWAP_FACE_MASKING_DEFAULTS.target` (`"head"`).
