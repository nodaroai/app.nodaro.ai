Competitor tracking (Nodaro Cloud): `@nodaro/shared` adds the tracked-brand, scan and action-card shapes, the card `kind` vocabulary and the scan price (`competitor-scan:<n>`, one Social Search page per search). `@nodaro/sdk` adds `client.competitors` (`list`, `get`, `create`, `update`, `delete`, `cards`, `discover`, `scan`) and re-exports the types. `@nodaro/cli` adds `nodaro competitors` (`list`, `add`, `discover`, `scan`, `cards`, `show`, `update`, `remove`).
