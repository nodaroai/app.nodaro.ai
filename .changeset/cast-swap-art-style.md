`CastSwapParams.direction?: { style }` — Flex's art style for a direct Nodaro Motion run (`style: "motion"`): a `style` picker-catalog id that restyles the whole video, worded by the platform. Refused with 400 `unknown_art_style`, `art_style_not_supported` (a saved moodboard) or `art_style_needs_flex` (Precise or a trend run); it never changes the price, and the job records it in `input_data.direction`.
