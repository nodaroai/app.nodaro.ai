A brand can hold up to 100 search terms (`COMPETITOR_SEARCH_TERMS_MAX`, was 15), and the new `COMPETITOR_SCAN_SEARCH_LIMIT` (100) is the most searches one scan runs. A plan's cap on terms is unchanged; with no cap, what bounds a brand is the size of its scan: one search per account, plus one per platform for each term.
