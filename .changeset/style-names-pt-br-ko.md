The art style catalog's Brazilian Portuguese and Korean translations now name every style. Brazilian Portuguese keeps the loanwords its speakers use ("Anime", "Noir", "Pixel art", "Pop art", "Concept art", "Cyberpunk", "Steampunk", "Vaporwave", "Low poly", "Ukiyo-e", "Glitch art") as explicit names, and gives "Animação em massinha", "Arte ASCII" and "Planta técnica" for claymation, ASCII art and blueprint. Korean adds "우키요에" and "아스키 아트".
