Competitors over time. Scans are kept for a window that follows the plan (`CompetitorListResult.historyMonths` on the list; `client.competitors.listWithPlan()`), every platform tally carries the account's `followers`, `client.competitors.get(id, { scan })` opens a brand as of one scan, `history(id)` (`CompetitorHistory`) is the brand's scans oldest first with what each found and read per platform, and `compare(id, { from, to, vsFrom?, vsTo? })` (`CompetitorCompareResult`) answers one or two periods: posts by publish date, usual reach, followers and their change, what worked, the best posts. The CLI gains `competitors history <id>` and `competitors compare <id> --from --to [--vs-from --vs-to]`.
