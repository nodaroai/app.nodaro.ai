`castSwap.styleChoice()` answers `styleOnly` per engine: whether a Flex run with an art style and nothing to swap restyles the whole clip there. Elsewhere on Nodaro Motion Prism an art style alone is refused, and one with a pre or post prompt runs as any other art-style run. It follows the engine alone: an engine answered `"precise"` drops the art style, so such a request is refused as `validation_error` and, with anything else to change, runs as Precise. `CastSwapStyleChoice` gains `styleOnly?: Partial<Record<CastSwapEngine, boolean>>`; Nodaro Motion Pulse is always `false` (it takes no art style), and an older server leaves it out (read it as `false` for every engine). Such a run (`style: "motion"` on Nodaro Motion Prism, a `direction.style`, no role with images and no fixed reference) restyles the whole clip, keeping its people, places, motion and sound. The doc comments of `CastSwapParams.roles`, `direction` and `inPreview` and the `no_roles_in_preview` refusal now say what the engine accepts: a role with images, a fixed reference, a pre or post prompt, or an art style. `CastSwapEstimate.breakdown` names `referenceAudio`, a part the server already answers when that step runs.
