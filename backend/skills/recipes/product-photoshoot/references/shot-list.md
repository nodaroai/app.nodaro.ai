# Shot List — nine ad archetypes

Each archetype is one `text-prompt` + `generate-image` pair sharing the
campaign reference. The skeletons use PRODUCT for the product noun ("trail
running shoe", "espresso machine") and "HEADLINE" for copy the user supplies —
rendered text stays short and in quotes. Reuse the reference's actual colorway
in every prompt.

## 1. Hero float

Premium PRODUCT advertisement. The PRODUCT floating dynamically on a vivid
solid-color background. Massive bold typography reading "HEADLINE" dominates
the composition. High-end marketing design, strong contrast, minimal layout,
dramatic studio lighting, sharp shadows, magazine-cover style, ultra-clean
commercial campaign.

## 2. Split-panel lifestyle collage

Modern PRODUCT advertising collage. Split-panel campaign featuring a confident
person pointing toward the PRODUCT. Bold white typography, two-color palette
matching the product, textured graphics behind the product, energetic
composition, social-media advertisement, high contrast, commercial
photography, dynamic layout.

## 3. Launch poster

Luxury PRODUCT launch poster. The PRODUCT displayed on a futuristic gradient
background. Large headline reading "HEADLINE". Premium infographic elements,
glowing panels, performance statistics, sleek modern typography, high-end
presentation slide, cinematic lighting, ultra-sharp product rendering.

## 4. Technical blueprint

PRODUCT technical breakdown poster. Industrial blueprint-inspired layout
featuring detailed diagrams, specifications, sketches, measurements, and
annotations surrounding the PRODUCT. Restrained engineering color scheme with
one accent color. Premium technology presentation, magazine editorial design,
highly detailed infographic.

## 5. Provocation ad

Aggressive marketing advertisement for the PRODUCT. White background with bold
two-color typography. Headline reading "HEADLINE" phrased as a challenge to
the reader. The PRODUCT positioned diagonally with motion graphics and
performance messaging. Minimalist commercial design, strong visual hierarchy,
clean modern layout.

## 6. Them-vs-us comparison

Competitive comparison advertisement. Split-screen design comparing two
products. Left side grayscale labeled "THEM", right side the vibrant PRODUCT
labeled "US". Feature comparison checklist, bold typography, dramatic
contrast, persuasive product positioning, modern infographic advertisement.

## 7. Luxury flat-lay

Luxury product flat-lay photography. The PRODUCT arranged among premium
accessories and lifestyle objects that fit its world. Dark monochrome
background with a selective accent color matching the product. Professional
commercial photography, top-down composition, ultra-detailed studio lighting.

## 8. Billboard in the wild

Massive urban billboard advertising the PRODUCT at night. Large digital screen
displaying the campaign with matching graphics. Pedestrians walking below in a
modern city environment. Cinematic atmosphere, realistic reflections, premium
outdoor advertising, commercial photography, shallow depth of field.

## 9. Exploded components

Exploded product design visualization of the PRODUCT. The product separated
into its individual components and structural layers. Clean white background,
engineering presentation style, premium product development showcase, highly
detailed 3D rendering, industrial design aesthetic.

## Choosing a set

- Social campaign: 1, 2, 5, 6.
- Launch or landing page: 1, 3, 4, 9.
- Brand-feel set: 1, 7, 8.
